Doyra Studio — three directions

A · Gumbaz

Daylight under a dome. The usul is the ring of glazed tiles around the dome's drum; bums send shockwaves through the mosaic, baks chip off shards. Library = row of iwan arches.

 B · Bazaar

A drum-maker's stall. Songs hang on the wall as painted doiras; the picked one swings down to the stage. The cycle is stitched around the rim; the crossfader is a suzani curtain.

 C · Beads

A poster that plays. Bone paper, ink and one lacquer red. Strokes are beads on a string that tightens where the baks crowd; the type swells with every stroke.
